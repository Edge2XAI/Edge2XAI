Hardware without the logistics business

Recommended hardware, not Edge2XAI inventory.

For hands-on packages, Edge2XAI can provide a tested bill of materials and links to compatible development boards, microphones, debug accessories, and related hardware. The goal is to reduce setup uncertainty without turning Edge2XAI into a warehousing, shipping, returns, or customs operation.

Tested BOM Compatibility guidance Vendor / distributor links Affiliate links where appropriate

Any future affiliate relationships should be disclosed clearly. Hardware would be purchased directly from the manufacturer or distributor, not fulfilled by Edge2XAI.

Implementation depth

More than a source-code download.

The reference repository is intended to make architectural decisions observable: how capture, buffering, DSP, quantization, inference, validation, and product behavior connect on real hardware.

reference-project/ ├── audio_capture/ ├── streaming/ ├── dsp_features/ ├── model_quantization/ ├── embedded_runtime/ ├── validation/ ├── debugging_rca/ └── reference_system/

The board is the laboratory

The architecture is what should transfer.

ESP32-S3 or another target may provide the concrete implementation, but the learning objective is to understand the contracts between acquisition, DSP, ML, runtime, validation, and product behavior so the same reasoning can move to other hardware and audio EdgeAI applications.

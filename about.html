My background is rooted in embedded systems: firmware, RTOS-based systems, device drivers, hardware/software integration, real-time constraints, debugging, and production engineering.

EdgeAI adds new layers — DSP, data pipelines, quantization, ML runtimes, model behavior, and lifecycle concerns — but it does not remove the engineering fundamentals underneath them.

Edge2XAI is being built around that intersection: helping experienced embedded engineers understand what changes, what remains familiar, and how the complete system fits together from physical signal to product behavior.

The Edge2XAI thesis

AI does not start at the model.

The model is one component inside an engineered system. Real products still depend on hardware, firmware, timing, memory, DSP, runtime integration, validation, observability, and lifecycle discipline.

Physical World→Hardware→Firmware / RTOS→DSP / Data→ML Runtime→Product Behavior

Direct contact

Keep the first message simple.

A few lines about your context, what you're trying to learn or solve, and what kind of help you're looking for is enough. There is no need to prepare a formal proposal before reaching out.

For a 1:1 guidance request, it helps to include:
  • Your background — roles, domains, and how long you have worked in embedded systems.
  • What you want to solve or learn — a project, a skill gap, or a capability goal.
  • Where you are now — what you have tried, and where you are stuck.
  • Your timeline — roughly when you would like to start and how much time you can commit.

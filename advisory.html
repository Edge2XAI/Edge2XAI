Selective by design

Selective by design: small scope, real engineering depth.

The purpose of this vertical is to leave room for useful technical engagements without creating an expectation that Edge2XAI can take ownership of a complete commercial product or provide a large engineering team.

Good-fit engagements should be technically bounded, aligned with demonstrated experience, and realistic within available time.

Potential engagement types

Small scope. Clear technical objective.

These describe the kinds of discussions Edge2XAI may selectively support as capability and availability permit.

01 · OUTPUT: ARCHITECTURE NOTES

Architecture Discussion & Review

Review system boundaries, HW/SW/ML partitioning, DSP/inference dataflow, runtime structure, resource constraints, and architectural alternatives.

02 · OUTPUT: A SECOND OPINION

Technical Review & Second Opinion

Independent review of an approach, prototype, architecture, learning project, or integration plan to identify questions, assumptions, risks, and possible alternatives.

03 · OUTPUT: A DEBUGGING PLAN

Debugging & Problem-Solving Session

Focused reasoning around a bounded Embedded Systems or EdgeAI issue where instrumentation, dataflow, runtime behavior, DSP/ML interaction, or integration needs another engineering perspective.

04 · OUTPUT: DIRECTION FOR YOUR PoC

Learning / Prototype Advisory

Guidance for individuals, small teams, student groups, or academic projects developing a PoC or reference implementation and needing architecture or implementation direction.

Advisory or mentorship? Mentorship grows an engineer over time. Advisory reviews a specific system or decision. If you want to build your own capability, see Training & Mentorship.
